8-Stage Platinum Roadmap

A structured, verified route through Leonida for 100% completion and platinum. Encodes PowerPyx & GTABase guidelines, including Chapter 1 missable warning and cheat behavior.

⚠️ Chapter 1 Missable: Meditate at Jason's house during Chapter 1 before finishing the chapter (Positive Mental Attitude) — the only reported missable in the game.
⚠️ Cheat Warning: Using cheat codes disables trophies AND saving (PowerPyx). Avoid cheats entirely.

Stage 1: Story & Campaign Progression

0/14 Earned

Chapters 1–4, final missions, and named operations. Free-roam continues after story completion (PowerPyx). No mission replay.

Stage 2: Stranger Strands & Encounters

0/6 Earned

Complete character strands including Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, and Tyrone finales, plus all 8 stranger strands across Leonida.

Stage 3: Collectibles & Exploration

0/7 Earned

Discover 3 card sets, WAiNK ×100, meditation spots, 100 vehicles, explore all Leonida districts, register 10 vehicles, and find Bret's unique animal.

Stage 4: Activities & Challenges

0/8 Earned

Master 250 stunt jumps, Shooting Range golds, drone races at all locations, 20 race wins, fishing vs Cal, arcade games, and 50 of 80 challenges (PowerPyx).

Stage 5: Free-Roam Feats & Combat

0/15 Earned

Execute partner wanted escapes, 3 shops back-to-back, 5 bank vaults, armored trucks, undetected jewelry heists, Combat Focus ×5, and gang operations.

Stage 6: Wealth & Enterprise

0/3 Earned

Launder $250,000 in illicit funds, spend $100,000 across Leonida luxury markets, and purchase all available garage properties.

Stage 7: 100% True Completion Mop-Up

0/1 Earned

Unlock 'A Long Road' (Gold). Reaching ~60% true checklist completion triggers final mop-up (~200 hours, difficulty 4/10 per PowerPyx).

Stage 8: Platinum Crown

0/1 Earned

Acquire 'Vice Got a Price!' by unlocking every other trophy across Leonida to claim Leonida Legend status.

Missables & Permanent Cautions

Detailed breakdown of the game's strict missable windows and permanent character profile cautions, backed by named guides.

Official Scope Note: Of the 55 trophies in Grand Theft Auto VI, exactly two carry explicit flags: Positive Mental Attitude (Missable in Chapter 1) and Actions Speak Louder (Permanent caution for Irredeemable Criminal Profile). These flags and windows are sourced directly from PowerPyx and GTABase.
⚠️ Strictly Missable Trophy

#31: You WAiNKer! silver

Description: WAiNK It at 100 locations.

Miss Window & Critical Warning

Chapter 1 Window: You must meditate at Jason's house during Chapter 1. Once Chapter 1 concludes and you progress to later chapters, this specific meditation location becomes permanently inaccessible. Missing it locks you out of this trophy until a new playthrough (PowerPyx / GTABase).

Guide & Steps

What it takes — Interacting with all 100 WAiNK locations scattered across the state of Leonida (PowerPyx). Where and when — Tackled during roadmap stage 3 comprehensive open-world exploration and collectible gathering (GTABase). Steps — 1) Locate WAiNK interactive spots marked on the world map (PowerPyx). 2) Perform the WAiNK interaction at all 100 unique sites (GTABase). 3) Track progress via the app ledger counter (Exophase).

⚠️ Permanent Character Profile Caution

#15: Cunning Stunts silver

Description: Complete 250 Stunts.

Permanent Irredeemable Criminal Profile

Caution: This trophy permanently modifies your career profile to 'Irredeemable Criminal'. While not missable in terms of a temporal window, it is an irreversible profile state once triggered across your save file. Players aiming for specific clean character playthrough stats should take note (GTABase).

Guide & Steps

What it takes — Executing 250 daredevil vehicle stunt jumps scattered throughout the Leonida map (PowerPyx). Where and when — Tackled during roadmap stage 4 across highways, rooftops, and rural ramps (GTABase). Steps — 1) Locate stunt jump ramps using the world map (PowerPyx). 2) Launch vehicles cleanly across designated landing zones 250 times (GTABase). 3) Track completed stunt tallies in the app ledger (Exophase).
